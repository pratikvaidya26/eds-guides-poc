About the InfoScale product suite

The InfoScale product suite addresses enterprise IT service continuity needs. They provide resiliency and software defined storage for critical services across a data center in physical, virtual, and cloud environments. The clustering solution provides high availability and disaster recovery for applications across geographies.
The InfoScale product suite includes the following products:
  • InfoScale Foundation
  • InfoScale Storage
  • InfoScale Availability
  • InfoScale Enterprise
