Permanent and keyless licenses are replaced by a universal, subscription-based license that aligns directly with your subscription entitlement's end-date.
While older InfoScale licensing models were non-enforcing and relied solely on notification messages, the new model actively tracks the license lifecycle.
By eliminating multiple license types based on core counts and platform-specific entitlements, InfoScale has simplified license management with a single subscription-based license key for all the variants of the InfoScale product. This shift to a single subscription license key reduces administrative overhead, making installation, renewal, and tracking significantly simpler while giving customers a clear, controlled timeline to manage renewals before any functionality is impacted.</p></body><topic class="- topic/topic " ditaarch:DITAArchVersion="1.2" domains="(topic hi-d) (topic ut-d) (topic indexing-d) (topic hazard-d) (topic abbrev-d) (topic pr-d) (topic sw-d) (topic ui-d)" id="understanding-the-license-lifecycle"><title class="- topic/title ">Understanding the license lifecycle</title><body class="- topic/body "><p class="- topic/p ">Previously, licenses were non-enforcing, only generating notification messages upon expiry while product functionality continued indefinitely. The new behavior actively enforces the license lifecycle, impacting functionality and transitioning the product through Warning, Grace, and Enforced states. A controlled enforcement of licenses ensures customers have sufficient time to renew licenses without service disruption.
